Storyline

Magic Happens in Every Single Choice You Make

A student sits at a desk with three clay fruits — a blueberry, an apple and an orange. She repeats the same simple action several times: arranging the fruits on the table. However, every small choice creates a different state in the world around her. When she places the blueberry first, the arrangement feels calm and balanced. When the apple is placed aggressively, the space becomes crowded and tense. When she hesitates before touching the orange, the rhythm slows and the lighting becomes dimmer. Although the fruits remain the same, each decision changes the atmosphere, pacing and visual composition of the scene. In the end, the student realizes that the world is not shaped by magic itself, but by the choices people make every day. The “magic” exists within human actions and decisions.

Design

Before production began, our team carefully planned the visual style of the animation. The clay fruits were selected as symbolic objects representing different choices and emotional states. The design process included:

• Character and scene planning
• Prop creation using clay
• Composition testing
• Camera angle exploration
• Colour and atmosphere planning
• Lighting experiments

These design decisions helped us communicate the emotional impact of each choice and supported the overall theme of the project.

Storyboard

The storyboard was developed before filming to organise the narrative and visual sequence of the animation. Each scene was carefully planned to determine:

• Character actions
• Fruit placement
• Camera positions
• Lighting changes
• Scene transitions

The storyboard provided a clear guide throughout production and helped ensure consistency between scenes.
